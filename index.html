Primary Care for Cancers

Practical, evidence-current learning for GPs, family medicine specialists and trainees, and for nurses, pharmacists and allied health professionals. Organised around what you do in the consultation: recognise, refer, share care, follow up, support and safety-net.

About this resource

Evidence policy

Malaysian guidance and data come first; WHO, NICE, USPSTF, ACS, ESMO and others are shown side by side and never merged. Every recommendation names its source, body and year, and expert opinion is labelled.

Author

Prof Boon-How Chew, Department of Family Medicine, Universiti Putra Malaysia. Builds on Chew & Taher (FMCH 2013; JFMCH 2014) and the ebook Primary Care for Cancers (v4R2025).

Privacy and use

No tracking, cookies or external requests. Progress is kept only in your browser. AI-assisted drafting and evidence checking under the author's direction; all content reviewed by the author.
